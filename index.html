Phase 1

The Toxic Introduction

As you inhale, hot gas (up to 900°C) blasts the delicate tissues of your mouth and throat. Formaldehyde, arsenic, and benzene immediately begin damaging cell DNA on contact.

Paralyzes Cilia: The tiny protective hairs in your throat are instantly immobilized, leaving lungs defenseless.

Phase 2

Alveolar Invasion

The smoke reaches the 500 million tiny air sacs (alveoli) in your lungs. It's here that oxygen is exchanged for carbon monoxide, starving every organ of vitality.

Tar

Coats the lungs in a sticky, black residue, permanently reducing elasticity and breath volume.

CO

Carbon monoxide displaces oxygen in red blood cells, forcing your heart to work twice as hard.

Phase 3

Systemic Distribution

Nicotine hits your brain in just 7 seconds, activating dopamine loops. But the carcinogens are now distributed *everywhere* via the bloodstream—affecting arteries, the heart, and reproductive organs.
