Chapter 02 // Micro-Physiology

Alveolar Gas Exchange

Deep inside the lungs, 300 million micro-sacs (alveoli) create a gas diffusion area equivalent to a tennis court ($70\,\text{m}^2$). Oxygen diffuses into capillaries while carbon dioxide is expelled.

Cellular Gas Diffusion Barrier

Membrane Thickness 0.5 Micrometers

Chapter 04 // Defense Collapse

Ciliary Paralysis

Microscopic cilia sweeping away debris are paralyzed within seconds by formaldehyde and acrolein. Toxic tar settles permanently in airway walls.

Chapter 05 // Systemic Pathology

Vascular & Cardiac Impact

Carbon monoxide displaces oxygen in hemoglobin, starving organ tissue. Simultaneously, nicotine elevates heart rate and hardens arterial blood vessels.

Clinical Disease Progression

  • COPD: Irreversible airway destruction.
  • Emphysema: Permanent rupture of alveolar walls.
Chapter 06 // Physiological Repair

The Capacity to Recover

Human tissues begin cellular repair immediately upon stopping smoking. Within 1 to 9 months, ciliary networks regenerate, clearing accumulated debris.

20 Minutes Blood pressure & pulse normalize
12 Hours Blood CO drops to normal range
1 Year Coronary heart disease risk drops 50%
