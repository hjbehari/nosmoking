Scientific Terminology

Clinical Glossary

Alveoli

Microscopic elastic air sacs where passive gas exchange between air and capillary blood occurs.

Cilia

Hair-like projections lining bronchial airways that beat rhythmically to sweep mucus and debris upward.

FEV1 (Forced Expiratory Volume)

Clinical measurement of air volume exhaled forcefully in the first second of exhalation.

Emphysema

Pathological destruction of alveolar walls resulting in enlarged air spaces and loss of elastic recoil.
