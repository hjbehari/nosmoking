Clinical Profiles

Pulmonary Disease Pathogenesis

COPD

COPD & Airway Obstruction

Progressive airflow limitation caused by small airway remodeling, inflammatory thickening, and loss of elastic lung recoil.

Ref: WHO Global COPD Report
EMP

Alveolar Emphysema

Permanent destruction of alveolar walls driven by elastase release from inflammatory neutrophils, reducing gas diffusion area.

Ref: NIH / National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute
CBR

Chronic Bronchitis

Submucosal gland hypertrophy causing mucus hypersecretion, productive cough, and chronic bronchial lumen narrowing.

Ref: CDC Smoking & Tobacco Use Evidence Base
